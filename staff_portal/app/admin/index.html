Phones and computers

Staff members

For shared phones: each person taps their name and enters their own PIN, so every action is recorded under their name. What they can control comes from the phone's role.

Roles

    Pick a role, or create one like “Maintenance” or “Housekeeping.”

    Phone layout

    Arrange what staff see: pages, group cards such as “Tub 1” that open to show everything about it, single devices, whole areas and headings. Each phone only sees the parts its role allows, and only gets controls where its role says Control, unless you give an item its own access (Edit → Access on phones). Every phone shows every page unless you choose otherwise (“Choose phones” on a page, or Phones → Edit). With no pages, phones use the automatic layout grouped by area.

      Full backup

      Everything in Staff Access in one file: settings, logo, areas, roles, layout, phones, staff and each phone's own arrangement. Restoring it puts all of that back, and phones that were already set up keep working. The activity log isn't included.

      Keep backup files somewhere private: they include staff and phone PINs (scrambled, never readable). Home Assistant sign-ins and pending setup QR codes are never included. Staff Access is also part of Home Assistant's own backups.

      Layout

      The phone layout: pages, group cards, dividers and access settings. Importing loads it into the Layout tab so you can check it before saving. Whole-area items are matched to your areas by name.

      Roles

      Every role with its device and area access. Importing adds them to your roles; areas are matched by name. You choose what happens to roles with the same name.

      Staff

      Staff members with their PINs (scrambled) and which shared phones they may use. Importing adds them; phones are matched by name. Home Assistant links aren't included.

      Areas

      Group devices the way your staff think about the property, such as “Soaking Area” or “Mechanical.” An area can include whole Home Assistant areas (new devices there join automatically) and individual devices. The staff app shows devices under these headings, in this order, and roles can grant access to a whole area.

      Branding

      A square PNG with some padding looks best. Without a logo, the icon shows your initials.

      Set up

      1. Open the phone's Camera and point it at this code.
      2. Tap the link, then follow the prompt to Add to Home Screen.
      3. Open the new icon. The phone stays signed in until you revoke it.

      Or open this link on the phone:

      1. On the computer, open this link in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. You can email it to yourself or type it in.
      2. It opens Staff Portal, signed in. To keep it handy, use the browser's Install app option (Chrome and Edge) or bookmark it.
      3. The computer stays signed in in that browser until you revoke it.

      Only open it on the computer you're setting up: whoever opens it first gets access.

      Link to a Home Assistant user

      Changes made from this phone will show in Home Assistant's logbook as this user, for example “Porch Light turned off by Maintenance.” The phone can still only use what its role allows.

      Use a non-admin user without two-factor login. The password is used once to sign in and isn't saved.
