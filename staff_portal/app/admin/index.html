Staff phones

Staff members

For shared phones: each person taps their name and enters their own PIN, so every action is recorded under their name. What they can control comes from the phone's role.

Roles

    Pick a role, or create one like “Maintenance” or “Housekeeping.”

    Phone layout

    Arrange what staff see: pages, group cards such as “Tub 1” that open to show everything about it, single devices, whole areas and headings. Each phone only sees the parts its role allows, and only gets controls where its role says Control. Every phone shows every page unless you choose otherwise (“Choose phones” on a page, or Phones → Edit). With no pages, phones use the automatic layout grouped by area.

      Areas

      Group devices the way your staff think about the property, such as “Soaking Area” or “Mechanical.” An area can include whole Home Assistant areas (new devices there join automatically) and individual devices. The staff app shows devices under these headings, in this order, and roles can grant access to a whole area.
