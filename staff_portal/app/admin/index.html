Departments

A department gives its people a role, the key card made for them with Make key card, and whether they must be clocked in to sign in. People linked to Connecteam are in every department whose smart group they're in. Someone in several departments gets each of these from the first one in this list that sets it, so drag to change the order.

A role decides what a kiosk device with that role shows, and what each person with that role sees when they sign in on a role-based device. People are listed under their role: click someone to change their access or pages just for them.

Roles

    Pick a role, or create one like “Maintenance” or “Housekeeping.”

    Wristbands

    Staff signed in on a front-desk computer make TT Hotel wristbands and key cards on the card encoder plugged into it. Set the defaults here, set each front-desk computer's card encoder (This computer (USB) is easiest) in → Edit, and choose who may make which cards on the tab. Every card is recorded with who made it.

    Staff cards
    Lost key cards: remote lock-out (experimental)

    A lost staff card keeps opening its doors until it expires. You can always lock it out by tapping a lost card notice or a replacement card on each door (Staff tab, Key cards). Remote lock-out does it through TT Hotel instead, for doors with a gateway, without walking to them.

    Experimental: TT Hotel doesn't document this for cards made on an encoder. A card number locked out remotely is retired on that door for good: that card can never open it again, even after it's cleared and made again. Test it on one door before using it.

    Time zone

    The front-desk computer's Windows time zone must be the same: the encoder works out each door's time from that computer's clock.

    Advanced

    Only change these if TT Hotel's own settings were changed, or the wristbands you use need it.

    Card encoders

    Each front-desk computer with an encoder runs TT Hotel's free SN Web Socket helper. With This computer (USB) (the default for new computers), Staff Access reaches the encoder through the staff app open on that computer, so it connects while someone signed in there has Wristbands open, and Staff Access never connects to it over your network. For other setups, Automatic looks for SN Web Socket on the networks in Home Assistant's network setting (or the ones entered under Advanced) and uses only the one you confirm with Use this encoder, or enter its Address. Between jobs the encoder is free for the TT Hotel program.

    Doors

    The doors TT Hotel lists for your property, by building and floor. Rename a door to what staff call it (TT Hotel keeps its own name), or hide it from staff lists. A hidden door can still be one of the day-pass or overnight doors above.

    Mark each door as a public door (such as the pool gate or the main entrance), a guest room or a staff door (such as the office or the boiler room). Staff pick overnight guests' rooms from the guest rooms, and shared doors from the public doors. A door not marked yet shows in both. Staff doors only go on staff cards and contractor badges.

    Door activity

    Who opened which door and how (card, app, passcode…), as TT Hotel reports it, newest first. Each key card's Last used comes from here. A lost or revoked card tried at a door is marked here and noted in Activity. Kept for 180 days.

    Tasks

    Everyone signed in with their own PIN has a Tasks list in the staff app: tasks for their department, for Everyone, and tasks given to them. Anyone can add a quick task and change a task's status. Only the person who made a task, or someone whose role has Manage tasks (on the tab), can edit or delete it.

    Two-way sync with Connecteam Quick Tasks

    Quick tasks made here go to the Connecteam board you choose for their department, for the person they're given to (when they're linked to Connecteam). Changes go both ways: title, notes, due date, who it's for, Done and the bin. Tasks made on those boards in Connecteam show up here for the board's department. Tasks from forms stay with their form.

    The Connecteam app in needs quick_tasks.read and quick_tasks.write (and forms.write for forms that follow a manager field). Test checks them.

      Tasks from Connecteam forms

      Each new submission of a form chosen here becomes one task, with its answers in the notes, for each department you tick (or Everyone). Only submissions sent after you choose a form become tasks, and none becomes a task twice. Changing a form's departments moves its open tasks too.

      Turn on Connecteam in first.

      Checking Connecteam

      How often the forms above and the synced boards are checked for new and changed tasks: often in business hours, less often the rest of the day. Times are in the property's time zone.

      Phone layout

      Arrange what staff see: pages, group cards such as “Tub 1” that open to show everything about it, single devices, whole areas and headings. Each phone only sees the parts its role allows, and only gets controls where its role says Control, unless you give an item its own access (Edit → Access on phones). Every kiosk device shows every page unless you choose otherwise (“Choose devices” on a page or group, or Phones → Edit); on role-based devices, people see their role’s pages (Roles tab). Put pages in a group, such as “Pool staff”, to give them out together. With no pages, phones use the automatic layout grouped by area. Preview shows your changes as a phone or computer would, before you save.

        Backup and transfer

        Full backup

        Everything in Staff Access in one file: settings, logo, areas, roles, layout, phones, staff and each phone's own arrangement, plus the activity log and the wristband and key card history. Restoring it puts the setup back, and phones that were already set up keep working. The log and card history are merged: entries from the file that aren't here yet are added, and nothing here is removed.

        Keep backup files somewhere private: they include staff and phone PINs (scrambled, never readable) and the activity log. Home Assistant sign-ins, pending setup QR codes and the TT Hotel and Connecteam secrets are never included. Staff Access is also part of Home Assistant's own backups.

        Migrate to another install

        Moves everything to another install of this add-on, for example when you switch Home Assistant from one add-on repository to another (Home Assistant treats that as a different add-on). Phones and computers stay signed in and nothing needs typing again: the file carries the whole setup, the activity log and card history, PINs, and the TT Hotel and Connecteam connections with their secrets.

        1. Here, click Create migration file. Save the file and write down the code shown with it.
        2. Stop this add-on: in Home Assistant, Settings → Add-ons → Staff Portal → Stop. Both use port 8787, so only one can run at a time.
        3. Install the new add-on and start it.
        4. Open the new one's Backup tab, click Restore from migration file…, choose the file and type the code.
        5. Check that phones, computers and this dashboard work.
        6. Uninstall the old add-on and remove its repository. The Cloudflare Tunnel needs no change.

        Only the code can open the file, and the file holds everything, secrets included: treat both like a password and keep them apart. A file can be restored within 24 hours of being made, and only onto a new install that has no staff, phones, computers, roles, areas, departments, card jobs or layout pages yet.

        HR admins

        HR admins see everyone and run the HR portal: roles, sign-ins and HR settings. Only you choose them, here. Two is best (you and your HR lead), so neither depends on the other. Each one scans their own setup QR code with their phone to set up their authenticator.

        Emergency controls

        For when HR can't: unlock someone, reset their HR sign-in (their authenticator and recovery codes go, and they're signed out everywhere), or sign everyone out. Turning the HR portal off is above.

        Connections

        The services Staff Access signs in to. Open one to change its details; each has its own Save button.

        TT Hotel

        In the TT Hotel program on the front-desk computer, sign in as the hotel's admin and open Settings → Integration. Copy the Client ID, Client Secret, username and password from there. The password there is already scrambled (32 letters and numbers): paste it exactly as TT Hotel shows it, never your real password.

        TT Hotel's free limit is 30,000 requests a month, counting every request. Staff Access never uses more than a quarter of that (7,500), and its automatic door checks stop at half of its share so making cards always has room. Making cards needs a fresh key for the encoder from TT Hotel: one request covers about 8 minutes of card-making. Door checks cost a request or two each, and only run while Wristbands is on and something uses the door list: a computer's card encoder, key cards made on This computer, or door activity.

        TT Hotel's Callback URL (in the same place) is for door activity: its address is under Door activity on the tab.

        Wristbands is off, so Staff Access doesn't use TT Hotel. Turn it on with Make wristbands and key cards on the tab.

        Connecteam

        In Connecteam, open your name → Integration Center → OAuth 2.0, click Create app and give it the users.read and time_clock.read scopes (they can't be changed later: for other scopes, make a new app). Copy the Client Secret straight away: Connecteam only shows it once, then paste its keys here. API access needs Connecteam's Expert plan or higher, and the time clock needs the Operations hub. Staff Access and the HR portal only ever read from Connecteam: no write permissions are needed.

        Connecteam limits API requests per account, shared with your other integrations and reports (Expert plan: 100 a minute and 10,000 a day). One check covers everyone and costs one request per time clock. Staff Access checks on that schedule only while someone must be clocked in, plus once when someone the last check didn't find clocked in signs in or uses a control (that check replaces the next scheduled one if it's within 20 minutes of it). Reading never checks. With one time clock that's about 24 requests a day plus a few at the start of shifts. Staff Access never uses more than 60 a minute and 9,000 a day (90% of your daily limit), and pauses on its own if your account runs low. The HR portal reads its people through this connection, inside that: at most 6 a minute and 500 a day, and none once Staff Access has used half of its day, so clock checks always have room.

        If Connecteam refuses a request, automatic checks stop, with Connecteam's own words below. They try again by themselves after 30 minutes, up to 3 times a day; Test connection shows each step.

          Google Drive (HR files)

          The HR portal keeps employee files in a Google shared drive. A small script in your own Google account does the work, so there's no Google Cloud project, key file or Workspace admin setting to change. The script can only use the shared drives you pick here, and only answers Staff Access.

          1. In Google Drive, create a shared drive named HR Employee Files. If you like, create a second one, HR Restricted, for I-9s and accommodation records, with only you and your HR lead in it.
          2. Click Copy the script.
          3. Go to script.google.com, signed in as the owner only (sign out of other Google accounts, or use a private window), and click New project. Delete what's there, paste the script and save it (Ctrl+S). If the editor doesn't respond, refresh the page.
          4. Click the gear (Project Settings) and tick Show "appsscript.json" manifest file in editor. Back in the editor, open appsscript.json, replace everything in it with the settings from Copy the settings file, and save. This turns on Google Drive for the script and asks for full Drive access.
          5. Choose authorize in the list at the top and click Run. When Google asks, tick Select all (or every box, including Google Drive) and click Continue. If it says the app isn't verified, click Advanced, then Go to your project. The log should end with lines starting OK.
          6. Click Deploy → New deployment. Choose the type Web app, set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone, then click Deploy.
          7. Copy the Web app URL, paste it below and click Save.
          8. Pick the shared drive (and the restricted one), click Save, then Test connection.

          Restricted files will go in a “Restricted” folder in the HR drive, which every member of that drive can open. A second shared drive keeps them apart.

            The HR portal is off. You can set this up now: it only keeps files in Google Drive once you turn it on (HR portal tab).

            If Google doesn't offer Anyone under Who has access, your Google Workspace doesn't allow it: ask whoever manages it to let you share web apps with anyone (see the user guide). Deploy from the owner's account, which isn't going away: the script runs as whoever deployed it.

            Security

            The PIN pad signs in by itself, without OK. Where every PIN that can sign in on a phone or computer has the same number of digits, it signs in at the last one. Otherwise it waits this long after the last digit (500 ms to start with; 200 to 5,000). Shorter is quicker, but too short signs in people who type slowly before they've finished, as a wrong PIN.
